      Fetch KTU Results

      This tool signs in to the KTU portal and reads your semester grade card. It bypasses the 504 gateway timeout by retrying until the portal lets it through, so you do not have to refresh the site yourself. A normal fetch takes seconds, and a slow one keeps retrying for a few minutes, so leave this page open. It is an unofficial student project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University.

      When you tap Fetch Results, your registration number and password are sent to this site's server. The server signs in to app.ktu.edu.in , opens the grade card page, and reads it, streaming every step back to this page, which is what you see in the log. The finished grade card opens on its own page.

      Your password is used once and thrown away when the request ends. It is never written to a database, a file, or a log, and this site does not save it in your browser, your session, or a cookie. The only thing kept is the grade card itself, in this tab's session storage, and it disappears when the tab closes.

      This project is open source, so you can check all of this yourself instead of taking our word for it. The scraper is a single file, api/fetch_results.py , and it is the only place your credentials are used. The page logic lives in src/features/results .

      Signing in from this server does mean your credentials pass through a third party host on the way to KTU. That host is Vercel, the well known cloud hosting company this site is deployed on, and it is the only third party involved. Your password exists in its memory for the length of one request and nowhere else. KTU advises students never to share their credentials, so if you would rather not, sign in on app.ktu.edu.in yourself.

      It says my credentials are wrong even though they are not.

      KTU periodically forces a password change on the portal. If it has been a while since you last changed yours, complete the change on app.ktu.edu.in first. Until then the portal rejects the login, and this tool reports it the same way it reports a wrong password.

      It stopped partway.

      Most fetches finish in a few seconds. When KTU's portal is struggling, the tool keeps retrying for up to about five minutes before it gives up. Nothing is saved when a fetch fails, so starting again is safe.

      It says too many requests.

      The site caps how many fetches can start from the same internet connection every minute. If several people are fetching through one connection at the same time, or you tapped a few times quickly, the extra attempts are turned away. Nothing is blocked for good, so wait a minute and try again.
